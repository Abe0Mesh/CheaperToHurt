Health Costs in America

How Protected Are Americans From a Medical Bill?

A single emergency room visit averages about $2,400. In some states, that bill would absorb far more of a typical month’s income than in others. This map shows where the financial shock of getting sick may hit hardest.

A Real Story Behind The Numbers

She begged strangers not to call an ambulance.

In June 2018 at a Boston subway station, a woman suffered a severe leg injury while trying to get off a train. Witnesses said she begged people not to call an ambulance because she feared the cost, even while she was in clear pain.

The moment went viral because it captured a hard truth: in a medical emergency, many Americans weigh treatment against debt. This map picks up from that question and shows where one ER bill could hit household budgets hardest.

Why this still matters

About 1 in 6 adults reported delaying or skipping some care due to cost in 2024.

Reported by The Boston Globe, summarized by TIME, and current access data from KFF (2026 update using 2024 NHIS).
